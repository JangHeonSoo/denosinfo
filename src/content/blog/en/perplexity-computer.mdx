The era of asking AI for answers is over. Now, AI just does the work.

On February 25, 2026, Perplexity AI unveiled Perplexity Computer. Put simply: you describe the outcome, the AI handles everything else.

---

## So, What Exactly Is It?

Not a search engine. Not a chatbot. Perplexity Computer is a multi-agent orchestration platform that autonomously executes entire complex workflows from start to finish.

Tell it to "generate a competitor analysis report," and the system breaks that goal into subtasks, assigns each one to the best available AI model, and runs them in parallel. One agent is doing web research while another processes the data and a third drafts the document. The coordination is entirely automatic. You can step away and handle other things.

The core mechanism: 19 different AI models (20 for Enterprise) working simultaneously. The main reasoning engine runs on Opus 4.6, deep research routes to Gemini, image tasks go to Nano Banana, video to Veo 3.1, lightweight speed tasks to Grok, and long-context recall to ChatGPT 5.2. No other mainstream AI tool currently takes this approach.

---

## "Everything Is Computer" — The Declaration

On March 11, 2026, at Perplexity's first developer conference Ask 2026, the company dropped a bold new vision: "Everything is Computer."

Not a search engine. Not a chatbot. The claim is that AI itself becomes your computer. The four simultaneous announcements that day show how serious they are:

- Computer for Enterprise: 20-model orchestration, integrated with 400+ apps including Snowflake, Salesforce, and HubSpot
- Computer in Slack: Type `@computer` in any Slack channel to trigger multi-model AI research and workflow execution
- Personal Computer: A locally running AI agent on a Mac mini, with direct access to your files, apps, and sessions — running 24/7

Personal Computer is the unusual one. Rather than relying purely on the cloud, it pairs an M4 Mac mini as a dedicated local server with Perplexity's secure cloud infrastructure. Fully remote-controllable, with user confirmation required for sensitive actions and a complete audit trail. CEO Aravind Srinivas called it "a digital proxy that never sleeps."

---

## Skills — The AI That Learns and Remembers

Perplexity Computer introduces Skills: reusable instruction sets that teach the AI how to perform a specific workflow once, permanently.

Define an "SEO blog post creation" workflow once, and whenever new topics come in, the system executes the same process automatically. Skills can also be shared between users — developers are already publishing and distributing their own Skills as downloadable files.

Paired with this is persistent memory. Perplexity now accumulates user preferences, frequently used keywords, and past project context across all conversations. Critically, this data is not used to train models — it exists only to personalize outputs for the individual user.

---

## The Competitive Landscape: Vertical vs. Horizontal

OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google are all building vertical ecosystems — their own models, their own pipelines. Perplexity is going the opposite direction: a horizontal orchestration layer that routes any task to whichever model does it best.

That's a bold bet. With no proprietary frontier model to compete with directly, Perplexity stakes its competitive moat on the orchestration layer itself — the judgment of which model to deploy, when, and how. Internal data from December 2025 showed Perplexity users already switching models frequently based on task type. That pattern became the thesis.

Compared to OpenAI Operator, Perplexity Computer still lags on raw autonomy and task-completion accuracy. But on price, accessibility, and model diversity, Computer holds the edge.

---

## Where Development Is Headed — Here's the Roadmap

The trajectory so far makes the direction unmistakable.

1. Hardware Expansion — Windows Support for Personal Computer 
Currently locked to Mac mini. Given the sheer size of the Windows PC market, Windows support isn't a question of if — it's when. Keeping it Mac-only limits reach too severely.

2. Beyond 400 — Industry-Specific Connectors 
400+ app integrations is a strong foundation, but real competitive depth requires vertical-specific connectors for healthcare, legal, finance, and other regulated industries. Expect targeted expansion.

3. Model Council at Scale 
The existing Model Council feature runs multiple frontier models in parallel and surfaces where they agree and disagree. As this gets more sophisticated, Perplexity stops being a "fast answer" tool and becomes a verified answer tool — directly relevant for high-stakes professional research.

4. Agent-to-Agent Collaboration via MCP 
The MCP connector already lives in Slack. The logical next step: multiple organizations' AI agents communicating with each other via shared protocols. Computer becomes the intermediary — a universal AI translation layer.

5. Tighter Audit Trails and Enterprise Governance 
SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, and HIPAA compliance are already in place. Future iterations will push toward more granular per-action audit logs, rollback capabilities, and configurable permission scopes for enterprise deployments.

---

## One Honest Assessment

The vision is genuinely ambitious. The execution, as always, will lag behind.

Hardware adoption friction, privacy regulation hurdles, OS integration complexity, and the basic human wariness of "AI accessing my files" — these aren't minor obstacles. "AI becomes the computer" isn't just a marketing line. It's a claim about a fundamental shift in how computing works. How fast and how smoothly that shift happens is the real question Perplexity Computer has to answer.
